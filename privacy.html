Privacy notice · Updated November 5, 2026

Calculations and storage

The calculator processes your inputs in your browser. It does not ask you to upload print files or create an account. When local storage is available, we save your most recent calculator settings, currency and optional project name on your device so they can be restored next time. Reset restores the example defaults. To remove all stored settings, clear this website's data in your browser settings.

Shared estimates

Sharing creates a URL with your calculator settings in its fragment (the part after #). This includes costs, rates, quantities and pricing choices. Your project name is excluded. Anyone receiving the full link can read those settings. URL fragments are not part of the initial HTTP request to the hosting server, but browser history, extensions and scripts running on the page can access them. Treat these links as information you have chosen to share. Opening one replaces your locally saved calculator settings.

Advertising and analytics

This site loads third-party HilltopAds banner advertising scripts served from shameful-farm.com. The first click on a quote, copy breakdown or share estimate button also attempts to open a sponsored offer on bold-consequence.com in a new tab or window while continuing the original feature. This happens at most once per page visit and may be blocked by your browser. Advertising providers and their partners may process your IP address, browser and device information, referring page, and interactions with advertisements, and may use cookies or similar technologies. You can close the top banner for the current page visit. Popups or new tabs may appear according to the provider's settings and browser permissions. Third-party scripts run on this page and can access its URL, including shared estimate fragments. Avoid sharing sensitive cost information in links. The calculator itself does not require cookies, and Ahrefs analytics has not been enabled. For the provider's data practices, see the HilltopAds privacy policy.

Hosting and downloads

Your hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses, requested pages and security logs, according to its own privacy policy. Quote downloads are generated in your browser. A printed or downloaded quote includes your project name, material, quantity and selling price, but excludes the internal cost breakdown. Copy breakdown includes your internal costs and profit, so review it before sharing.

Your choices

You can use the calculator without sharing a link, avoid entering a project name, and disable or clear local storage. The calculator continues to work for the current visit when saving is unavailable.
